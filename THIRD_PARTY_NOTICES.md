## McBopomofo

`table/bopomofo_char.json` contains data derived from the single-character
Bopomofo mappings in McBopomofo's `Source/Data/BPMFBase.txt`.

Upstream project: <https://github.com/openvanilla/McBopomofo>

The source data was converted to JSON for use by ime-core, and fields not used
by ime-core were omitted.
